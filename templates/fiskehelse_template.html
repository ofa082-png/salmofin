Mortality from Fiskeridirektoratet's monthly biomass statistics (salmon, ~7 weeks lag). Tonnes = dead fish × mean weight of their generation that month. Rate = 1 − e−dead ÷ average stock, the Veterinary Institute's formula on pooled area data. Silage estimate: silage vessels collect dead fish, so dead tonnes per month = a + b × silage visits per day, fitted per region on all reported months since January 2024 (when the visit data starts). The error shown is leave-one-month-out. Indicator = silage visits ÷ feed-carrier visits (AIS, our fleet list), the same ratio as on the mortality page. ISA and PD from BarentsWatch weekly disease status. Current cases from Mattilsynet's public API: the counts are a snapshot, not a period. West = PO1–4, Mid = PO5–7, North = PO8–13; Norway includes sites without a production area.
