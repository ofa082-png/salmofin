Source: Fiskeridirektoratet biomass statistics by production area and generation (salmon; trout only in MTB use); Aquaculture Register; sea temperature from BarentsWatch lice counts. West = PO1–4, Mid = PO5–7, North = PO8–13; Norway includes rows without production area.
Growth at sea = Δ biomass + harvest + mortality biomass − stocked biomass (residual, so the balance closes by definition). Stocked biomass = fish per weight class × estimated class weight. Biological FCR = feed ÷ growth at sea. eFCR = feed ÷ (Δ biomass + harvest − stocked biomass); always ≥ biological FCR, the gap is mortality. TGC = 1000 × (W₁⅓ − W₀⅓) ÷ (°C × days), W in grams, by generation and PO; range from uncorrected to harvest-corrected start weight.
*MTB use = (salmon + trout) ÷ sea-site licence MTB (excl. slaughter pens and licences without PO); indicative. **>500 g = unreported stocking from the count balance.
Changes from previous edition: class-based stocked biomass (was [reported + other losses] × 0.50 kg); other losses (ANDRE_NY_STK) treated as a loss; regions PO1–4/5–7/8–13; trout included in MTB use; two FCR definitions; temperature and TGC; stocking mix, count balance and turnover sections.
