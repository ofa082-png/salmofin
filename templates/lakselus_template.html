Lice treatments per week

Sites reporting each type of treatment in the week. A site using two methods in a week counts twice. The method is only recorded from 2024 week 10; earlier non-medicinal treatments show as unspecified.

Why autumns differ

Delousing-vessel visits per week

Visits by delousing vessels in our fleet list to farm sites (AIS). Descriptive only: these vessels account for about a quarter of registered treatments.
Lice = mean adult female lice per fish across sites that counted that week (sites marked as probably without fish left out). A week is shown once at least 90% of the usual number of sites have reported; treatments and vessel visits stop at the same week. Normal = geometric mean for each week since 2017 (traffic-light era), excluding the current year. Outlook starts from how far the latest week sits from its normal. One method keeps that gap, the other lets it fade at the rate seen since 2017 and adds the sea-temperature anomaly, which also fades. The range covers both plus the typical backtest error (each past year forecast from the same week with that year left out). Up to 12 weeks ahead. Source: BarentsWatch weekly lice counts, sea temperatures and treatments; BarentsWatch AIS for vessel visits.
