Flags
Flags as of the latest complete week. Over lice limit: above the limit in any of the last 4 weeks. Near limit: latest count at 70% of the limit or more. Lice rising: latest count at least 0.25 and twice the average of weeks 5–8 before. New ISA or PD: suspected or confirmed in the last 6 weeks with no case in the 6 months before. Many treatments: treated in at least 3 of the last 4 weeks. Mechanical or thermal: in at least 3 of the last 6 weeks. Silage spike: 3 or more silage-vessel visits in 4 weeks and at least 2.5× the site's usual rate this cycle. Harvest started: a processing-vessel visit in the last 2 weeks after none in the 8 weeks before, on a cycle older than 6 months. Just stocked: cycle started within 8 weeks. Just emptied: fish last counted 2–8 weeks ago. Cycles = runs of weeks with lice counts and fish, allowing gaps up to 6 weeks, labelled by the year and half-year of the first week. Treatments: M mechanical, T thermal, F freshwater, B bath, I in-feed, O other, N non-medicinal (method not recorded). Vessel visits (our AIS fleet list) start in 2024. Company = licence holder with the most capacity on the site today. Source: BarentsWatch lice counts, treatments, disease status and AIS; Fiskeridirektoratet registers.
