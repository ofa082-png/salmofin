Tonnes of feed per visit

Sources: feed-carrier visits to sites from BarentsWatch AIS (vessels in our fleet list); feed consumption (salmon and trout) from Fiskeridirektoratet's monthly biomass statistics, published ~7 weeks after month end.
Model: feed per day = a + b × visits per day + seasonal harmonic + trend, fitted on all full months with both series. The same per-day formula converts daily visits into weekly tonnes. A partial month is estimated once at least 7 days of visits exist, with the remaining days projected from the weekday pattern of the last 8 weeks. Week forecast uses the average within-week pace of the last complete weeks.
