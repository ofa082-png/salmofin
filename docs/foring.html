Feed conversion (salmon)

Biological FCR = feed ÷ growth at sea (adds mortality, removes stocked biomass). eFCR = feed ÷ (Δ biomass + harvest). Same method as the control report.

Feeding rate and sea temperature

Feeding rate follows temperature; a year above the usual line at the same temperature means fish are being fed harder.

Tonnes of feed per visit

Sources: feed consumption, biomass, harvest and mortality from Fiskeridirektoratet's monthly biomass statistics (~7 weeks lag); feed-carrier visits to sites from BarentsWatch AIS (vessels in our fleet list), assigned to regions by the site's production area; sea temperature from lice counts. West = PO1–4, Mid = PO5–7, North = PO8–13; Norway includes rows and visits without production area.
Feed totals include salmon and trout (feed vessels serve both); FCR and feeding rate use salmon only. Visit model: feed per day = a + b × visits per day + seasonal harmonic + trend, fitted per region on all full months with both series. A partial month is estimated once at least 7 days of visits exist. Week forecast uses the within-week pace of the last complete weeks.
